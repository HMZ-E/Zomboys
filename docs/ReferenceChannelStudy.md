# Reference channel study

Research date: 18 October 2026, Africa/Casablanca. Reference: the official [Zombies Aren’t Real channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC6WmLGygZ8gSUf1NVhqqHwg), its linked developer walkthroughs, the [developer’s press kit](https://www.jogamedev.com/zar-presskit.html), and the [official Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5185890/Zombies_Arent_Real/).

This records the public evidence used to guide Zomboys. The channel recheck on **18 October at 18:59 UTC** found **15 uploads: one announcement trailer and 14 Shorts**. The original 14 were reviewed through captions and keyframes across their beginnings, middle sections and endings. The newly published fifteenth upload was reviewed through ten keyframes across its 39 seconds; YouTube reported no exportable transcript, so no spoken script is attributed to it. Three linked developer walkthroughs provide longer explanations. This is a study of published footage and descriptions, not access to the reference game’s source code, complete rule set or an independently played build. It does not establish exact numerical parity.

## How to read the evidence

- **Direct description** means the developer’s narration, captions or official text explicitly states the behavior.
- **Visible observation** means it can be seen in sampled footage. A frame can establish a wall, crowd or effect, but cannot establish an exact algorithm or a continuously maintained frame rate.
- **Implementation interpretation** means an original Zomboys design chosen to produce a comparable result. It must not be presented as a discovered proprietary rule.
- Timestamps below are approximate navigation points, rounded to the relevant passage. They are not full transcripts. Video titles and durations identify the reviewed uploads; current channel counts or titles can change.

## Design and movement findings

| System | What public evidence supports | Application to Zomboys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core loop | Plan, start the outbreak, adapt a finite defense, assess civilian survival, then retry with a changed strategy. Official text explicitly describes a non-incremental game. | Make planning, pause, same-seed retry and readable results central. Avoid a repeated-loss farming requirement. |
| Zombie pursuit | Footage shows dense streams splitting around city blocks, converging toward people and compressing at obstacles. One breach can seed another population. The longer walkthrough explicitly distinguishes exposed and protected prey. | Give pursuit a local, legible target and make walls, corners and open routes matter. A protected civilian remains a potential objective after exposed prey disappears. |
| Crowd motion | Crowds appear as small, continuously moving markers with branching flow and local congestion. Packed groups are visibly different from sparse streets. | Smooth movement and turning, lateral street occupancy and separation should produce continuous flow. This is an implementation goal, not proof that the reference uses boids, flow fields or any particular steering formula. |
| Civilians | Civilian accessibility changes with safe zones, walls and gates. Walls can trap the very people they protect. | Evacuation routes and civilian-only passage should be visible and actionable. Preserve gradual local evacuation knowledge requested for Zomboys. Do not give every civilian global instant knowledge merely because the reference shows a queue. |
| Defenders | Different range/fire-rate roles exist. Troops are vulnerable humans who can add to the outbreak when overrun. Rooftop height affects sniper range. | Make placement and retreat matter, retain building-blocked direct fire, and account for casualties and conversions consistently. Snipers remain the requested replacement for garrisons; higher positions should provide a readable benefit without firing through taller intervening buildings. |
| Streets and barriers | Streets, narrow passages, walls and gates determine where crowds accumulate. Gates cost resources as part of the same defense budget. | Keep drawn finite-durability barriers and paid repairs. Distinguish civilian access from infected blockage and vehicle access; show passage status rather than hiding it in rules. |
| Protected-area tradeoff | The newest Short visibly compares protected civilians, land coverage and per-cell wall cost. Its title frames a survival-versus-fortification tradeoff; no transcript was available. | Paid street closures and real safe-zone occupancy make this tradeoff actionable. Do not copy the clip’s displayed prices, percentages or polygon editor as though they were universal rules. |
| Support abilities | Paratroopers, stun gas, explosive ordnance, mines and C4 are publicly identified. Aircraft have readiness and a directional targeting step; bombs destroy structures. | Preserve paid tactical reserves and directional aircraft commands. Explosive tools need previews, delayed resolution where applicable, and civilian/building consequences. Exact costs and blast formulas remain original tuning. |
| Level design | Campaign scope expands geographically while earlier defenses persist. Denser populations and more entrances increase the area to protect. | Use staged districts inside Casablanca and Settat. Retain the requested city roster; importing the source game’s other cities is unnecessary to reproduce the strategic progression. |
| Results and learning | Civilian survival ranks and death/breach flags give feedback. Mission-specific thresholds can be demanding even at low survival percentages. | Show a useful after-action explanation and where a defense failed. Do not silently copy one video’s thresholds to all Moroccan missions. |
| Loadout | Improvements and active abilities compete for points; strategic reassignment is emphasized. | Offer meaningful tradeoffs and affordable experimentation. Persistent ownership and mission-specific choices should be explained separately. |
| Presentation | The sampled footage consistently uses pale civilians, green infected, blue protected areas, tan roofs and dark streets. Combat and danger stand out against subdued terrain. | Zomboys now uses white civilians and bright green infected overhead, olive defenders, amber exposure/incubation and blue protected districts. Original green/gray infected clothing distinguishes street-level bodies. |

## User requirements that take precedence

Studying the reference does not cancel the user’s explicit direction. The following remain Zomboys requirements even where the footage differs:

- **Only Casablanca and Settat.** Preserve their offline real-world street networks, original 3D buildings and landmark interpretations.
- **Kill money, no regenerative budget.** Retain money earned per confirmed kill and the one-time emergency help budget. The reference’s safe-zone income site is therefore excluded.
- **Large exterior arrivals.** Preserve visible assembled herds entering from outside the city, randomized route/arrival information and sufficient planning time. The reference’s small uncertain internal outbreak is not substituted for this system.
- **Manageable difficulty.** Preserve the requested slower infection/contact progression and affordable commands while increasing strategic consequences through positioning and access, not arbitrary speed.
- **Ground roles.** Keep spread rifle squads, a moving armed Humvee, indirect mortar fire and sniper teams. Preserve street scale and building-blocked sightlines.
- **Aircraft.** Keep the requested AC-130, B-2 and A-10 interpretations, their visible approaches, directional attack mechanics and collateral consequences. The A-10’s sustained BRRRT and natural audio tail remain essential.
- **Sound.** Preserve varied team weapon reports and unit-associated engines/effects, with the constant airplane-like background and zombie/civilian voices removed as requested.
- **Phone performance.** Keep bounded active simulation, batched distant markers and close-detail limits. The current 1,400-active-infected cap plus visible staged exterior members is an explicit mobile adaptation. A promotional claim of 500,000 NPCs does not establish that an iPhone can run 500,000 fully active agents, or that Zomboys does so.

## What remains unknown

The videos do not disclose source code, navigation graph construction, perception budgets, steering weights, contact-resolution order, infection probabilities, animation interpolation, exact weapon statistics, all upgrade branches or all mission data. Smooth motion is visible; its implementation is not. A tightly packed queue does not prove physical crowd simulation. A large population title does not prove every member runs full AI on each frame.

Zomboys uses original procedural assets, authored code and its own balancing for these systems. Its behavior can be compared against visible reference situations, but hidden-rule equivalence must not be claimed. The [v6 validation record](V6RenderValidation.md) is historical measured evidence; new gameplay changes require their own checks before inheriting a performance or correctness claim.

## Primary text cross-check

The [press kit](https://www.jogamedev.com/zar-presskit.html) identifies JoGameDev and Brad Clark and corroborates the planning/containment premise. [Steam’s official description and developer Early Access answers](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5185890/Zombies_Arent_Real/?l=spanish) corroborate specialized troops, evacuation, destructive support, upgrades and custom geographic maps. The [developer announcement on the Spellmasons hub](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1618380/) independently describes large simulated populations. These establish public design intentions, not the undisclosed mechanics listed above.

No developer message was sent, account changed or community post published during research. The [public Steam discussion listing](https://steamcommunity.com/app/5185890/discussions/) was inspected, but no inaccessible reply was treated as verified evidence.

## Zomboys implementation and verification

The following changes have been integrated into source during this study. The 24–25 October gameplay, interaction and phone measurements are recorded in [V7StrategyValidation.md](V7StrategyValidation.md). Those checks establish the documented behavior and measured performance; a systematic visual match of crowd motion and animation against the reference footage has not been completed.

| Applied system | Current Zomboys behavior | Deliberate limits or differences |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Safe zones and gates | Drawn districts purchase actual street closures. Civilian gates provide selective single-file admission; wall rendering leaves matching physical apertures. Protected population is counted from actual residents. Blue ground tint and boundaries identify zones; breached boundaries change color. | No passive safe-zone income. A drawn rectangle is a defense plan rather than a guarantee that every resident survives. |
| Mines and C4 | Proximity mines and manually triggered C4 have arming time and destructive blast areas. Original street models and distinct distant markers identify them. | Costs, timing, blast radii and collateral rules are original balancing. |
| Troop vulnerability and rooftops | Human crews can be lost and infected. Rooftop snipers receive height-related range and sightline treatment; models, crew poses and distant markers use their actual elevation. | No garrison roster is restored. Roof hosts, visibility and crew placement are covered by the V7 gameplay and interaction checks. |
| Expanding encounters | A district campaign uses an explicit catchment and carries surviving defenses into a larger encounter; the exterior-herd operation remains available. A dashed map boundary identifies campaign scope. | Only Casablanca and Settat. The catchment boundary is not an invisible defensive wall. |
| Failure feedback | Bounded casualty and breach records produce small persistent flags during and after an encounter. Nearby events aggregate. | The renderer draws at most 96 recent records in a cached, viewport-culled mesh; it does not create a scene node per casualty. |
| Crowd readability | Overhead roles use white, bright green, olive and amber with smooth disc edges; original articulated people appear at close tilted zoom. | Active AI, close-detail counts and GPU buffer pools stay bounded for iPhone. No 500,000-active-agent claim. |
| Existing requested systems | Kill money, one-time emergency funding, exterior herds, social evacuation, drawn durable barriers, Humvee patrols, delayed mortars and AC-130/B-2/A-10 support remain. | No regenerative budget, unwanted city roster, constant aircraft background or civilian/zombie voices. |

Six render-check groups passed in `.build/v7-render-checks.log`: actual Metal output confirms the new role colors, continuous three-pose interpolation, mesh reuse and ghost clearing, pause, distant-camera disc visibility, finite human vertices, and real gate openings including overlapping cuts. Source parsing passed for the renderer changes. The V7 validation record contains the final 327-check gameplay run, 13 distinct interaction flows passed across runs and the measured overhead phone workloads. At the user’s request, testing concluded with those collected phone measurements; the additional close-up 3D profile was not performed.

Exact proprietary algorithms, numerical balance and unpublished content remain unknown. Matching the visible design principles is the objective; this record does not claim a source-identical game.

## Movement and animation application

The 25 October motion pass applies additional findings directly to the app: bounded acceleration and turning, bend-aware pace, local pack flow, converging approach lanes, blended speed-dependent gaits, individual phase offsets, grounded feet and stable close-body selection in dense crowds. Staged exterior members can now appear as bodies within the existing detail budget. The implementation, actual geometry checks, paid battles and native recordings are documented in [V8MotionValidation.md](V8MotionValidation.md).

This is an applied motion pass with original code and animation cycles. A complete quantitative comparison of every reference sequence and source-identical steering or animation data remains outside the established evidence. Frame-rate and gameplay tests do not establish exact visual equivalence.
