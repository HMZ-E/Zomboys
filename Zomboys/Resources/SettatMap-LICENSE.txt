Settat extract produced 2026-10-11 with scripts/import-settat.py.

The complete derived database is distributed as SettatMap.json. It retains
georeferenced public-road centerlines, building footprints and parks inside
32.968–33.044 N, 7.668–7.576 W, covering Settat's city core, university area
and rural road approaches. This is a compact playable extract, not a legal
administrative-boundary map. Only the largest connected public-street graph
is navigable; isolated visual streets remain visible.
Known sections of long regional roads are retained despite distant references
outside the extract. Navigation edges crossing the playable square are clipped
at its boundary, preserving the exterior approach roads.

Buildings use OSM heights or building levels where tagged. Missing heights
are deterministic visual estimates. Custom landmark models are original
game silhouettes with approximate dimensions, not surveyed architectural
reproductions. The game uses no satellite or map imagery.

Projection: equirectangular at 33.006 N, 0.35 world units per meter, north up.
x = 100 + (longitude + 7.668) * 111320 * cos(33.006°) * 0.35
y = 100 + (33.044 - latitude) * 111320 * 0.35
Playable square: 3,200 world units per side.

Reproduce the selector thumbnail with scripts/create-map-preview.swift settat
after importing the database. Landmark source notes: docs/SettatMapNotes.md.
