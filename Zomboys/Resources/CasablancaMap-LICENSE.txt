This game distributes the derived Casablanca database in full as
CasablancaMap.json. Its road centerlines, building footprints, coastline,
and park locations are georeferenced OpenStreetMap data. Heights are used
from OSM height/building:levels tags where available; missing heights are
estimated for visual presentation. Custom landmark models are authored
interpretations and are not survey-quality reproductions.

The offline extract covers the connected Casablanca urban area, from the
western Corniche/Ain Diab through Sidi Bernoussi and Sidi Moumen, and south
to Lissasfa and Sidi Maarouf (33.47–33.67 N, 7.79–7.43 W). It is not a legal
administrative-boundary map of Greater Casablanca. Geometry is simplified
for real-time iPhone rendering; isolated private/disconnected streets are
not part of the simulation's connected public-street routing network.

Reproduction script: scripts/import-casablanca.py
CasablancaPreview.json is a simplified thumbnail derived from the same
database under the same license. Reproduce it with
scripts/create-map-preview.swift after importing CasablancaMap.json.
Game-space projection: equirectangular at 33.57 N, 0.235 world units/meter,
north up; x = 75 + (longitude + 7.79) * 111320 * cos(33.57°) * 0.235,
y = 800 + (33.67 - latitude) * 111320 * 0.235.
