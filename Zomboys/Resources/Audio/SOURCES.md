# Sound sources

- `a10_brrrt.wav`: complete gun-run report and its decay, excerpt 63.80–66.72 seconds from **The Gun**, Rachel Barton / 127th Wing Public Affairs, Selfridge ANGB, May 15, 2014, DVIDS video 337352 / VIRIN 140515-F-NQ307-001. https://www.dvidshub.net/video/337352/gun . DVIDS labels the source public domain. Mono 22.05 kHz conversion, gentle 28 Hz high-pass, peak normalization, 650 ms decay fade and 160 ms silent endpoint padding (3.08 seconds total); no speech, images, logos, or music excerpted. Public-use terms: https://www.dvidshub.net/about/copyright . The appearance of U.S. Department of War visual information does not imply or constitute Department of War endorsement.
- Remaining WAV sounds: original procedural sound design generated by `scripts/create-audio.py` for Zomboys. No third-party recording sampled. Aircraft engine loops and 30/105 mm cannon effects are designed game sounds, not authentic recordings of those aircraft. `rifle_0` through `rifle_3` and `sniper_0` through `sniper_3` use distinct crack, report, reflection and mechanism envelopes; each deployed unit keeps its assigned variation. `bomb_fall` is a continuous air rush/whistle whose volume and pitch follow the projectile; the impact has a layered blast and debris tail.

The user's four YouTube links are style references only. See `docs/AircraftReferenceNotes.md` for page titles, visible observations and access limitations. None of their uploaded media is downloaded or bundled.

The large source video/PCM/archive are development intermediates under `.build` and are not bundled in the application. The genuine GAU-8 excerpt is bundled offline and given priority in the bounded stereo mixer. The continuous city ambience, zombie groans/attacks and civilian scream assets have been removed. Engine loops use continuous periodic components without recurring fades to silence. Mixer pitch and pass envelopes follow aircraft motion relative to the camera. Aircraft and vehicle sound only exists for active flights/moving vehicles; there is no idle background loop. A playing GAU-8 burst cannot be evicted or restarted by a repeated preview tap. Pausing lets fired reports finish, while loops retire with a 200 ms volume ramp; mute/background/reset gently fade all sound before stopping playback.
